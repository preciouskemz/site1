WELCOME TO AHUDIYANNEM
FOOTBALL CLUB

Ahudiyannem Football Club, founded by Dr. Robert Daniel Onyeani, is a Nigerian football club based in Abiriba, Abia State. The club has rapidly risen through the ranks of Nigerian football, with the men's team competing in the NLO 2026 (Nationwide League One) and the women's team (Ahudiyannem Queens) competing in the NWFL Premiership — the highest women's league in Nigeria.

The men's team are the champions of the 2025 Abia State Presidential Cup (State FA Cup), a historic achievement that marked the club as one of Abia's brightest football prospects. They now compete in the NLO 2026, flying the flag of Abia State in the Nationwide League.

The women's team, Ahudiyannem Queens, gained promotion to the Nigeria Women Football League (NWFL) Premiership in 2025, competing against the best teams in the country. They are the reigning champions of the Abia State FA Cup (Women), having retained their title as back-to-back champions of the Abia State Presidential Cup for the second consecutive year. Known for resilience, teamwork, and an unbreakable fighting spirit, Ahudiyannem FC continues to grow and make its mark on Nigerian football.
